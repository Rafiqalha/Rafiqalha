I am an Informatics Engineering undergraduate at **UIN Maulana Malik Ibrahim Malang**, based in Malang, Indonesia. I work across AI applications, backend development, and product engineering, with a focus on turning real problems into useful product experiences.

My ambition is to build products that help people participate more confidently in the digital economy: farmers accessing practical guidance, students discovering better ways to learn, and readers examining the sources behind AI-assisted answers.

## Selected products & projects

| Project | Product vision | Explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ATSAR** | Help learners, researchers, and builders explore Islamic knowledge with traceable sources and a clear distinction between original references and AI-generated explanation. Starting with SANAD MCP. | [Product website](https://atsarai.com/) |
| **Nexus** | Explore identity verification that makes digital trust easier for product teams to integrate. An early-stage SDK initiative with ongoing development and validation. | [Public developer preview](https://github.com/Rafiqalha/nexus-ai-sdk) |
| **Scout OS / Khasyaraka** | Turn Scout education into engaging missions and practical learning journeys that connect knowledge with action. | [Repository](https://github.com/Rafiqalha/khasyaraka_app) |
| **Central Melon** | Explore how digital tools can support agricultural workflows and connect farming information with agribusiness needs. Developed as a collaborative student project. | [Repository](https://github.com/Rafiqalha/centralmelon-smartfarm) |
| **EconoMind AI** | Help students explore how data can support pricing decisions through a learning prototype developed within DSE's IT Incubation program. | [Repository](https://github.com/Rafiqalha/dse-economind-ai) |
